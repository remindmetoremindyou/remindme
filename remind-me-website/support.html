Common questions

Alerts don't fire when the app is closed

Open iPhone Settings → Remind Me → Location and choose Always. Also make sure Notifications are turned on for Remind Me, and that Low Power Mode and Focus modes aren't silencing alerts.

The alert comes a little late

In the app, open Settings (the gear) and choose Precise under Alert accuracy. This uses GPS and more battery. Battery saver mode relies on iOS region monitoring, which can be a few hundred feet late.

I can't find a store when I search

Try the store name with the city, such as “CVS Asheville”. If it still doesn't appear, tap the map to drop a pin where the store is and tap “Pin it”.

I don't hear the alert sound

Check that your iPhone's ring/silent switch is not set to silent and that the volume is up. Focus modes can also silence alerts.

How do I edit or delete a reminder?

Tap a reminder in the list to view or edit it. Press and hold a reminder to delete it.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. We don't have accounts or servers that hold your reminders. Deleting the app removes its data. See the Privacy Policy.

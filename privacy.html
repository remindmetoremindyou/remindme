Privacy Policy

Remind Me: Location Reminders · Effective October 11, 2026

The short version: your reminders and the places you pin stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

This policy explains how the Remind Me: Location Reminders app (“the app”) handles information. The app is published by an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

What stays on your phone

  • Your reminders, including titles, notes about where an item is, dates and times, chosen sounds, and settings.
  • The places you pin, including store names and map coordinates, and the home location you set for “Left behind” reminders.
  • Your preferences, such as light or dark theme and alert accuracy.

All of this is stored only on your device. We have no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Location

The app asks for location access so it can alert you when you arrive near a pinned place or leave home. Matching your position to your pins happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to us.

  • For alerts to work when the app is closed, the app needs the “Always” location setting. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Remind Me → Location.
  • With “Precise” alert accuracy turned on, the app uses GPS in the background, which uses more battery. iOS shows a blue indicator while this is active. You can switch to “Battery saver” in the app’s Settings.

Searching for stores

When you type in the store search box, the text you type and your approximate location (used to show nearby results first) are sent over the internet to a place-search service so it can return matches:

  • Apple Maps search (the same data used by the Maps app). Apple’s handling is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • If Apple Maps search is unavailable or finds nothing, the app may instead use the free Photon (photon.komoot.io) and Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) services, which are based on OpenStreetMap data. These services receive the search text, approximate location, and your IP address like any website you visit. They are operated by third parties under their own policies.

These searches are not linked to any account, because the app has none. The maps you see in the app are provided by Apple Maps.

Notifications

Alerts are created and shown by your phone (local notifications). They are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings.

Purchases

If the app offers paid features or subscriptions, payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking, and we do not follow you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools that identify you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any reminder or pinned place inside the app.
  • Turn off location or notifications at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its stored data from your phone.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
